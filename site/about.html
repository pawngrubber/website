I got the handle from a chess habit: taking the pawn, accepting the mess, and working it out over the board.

That's roughly how I build software too. I'd rather ship something rough and real, look at it with other people, and fix what actually breaks than plan a perfect thing in a document nobody reads.

Day to day I work on software tooling — agent orchestration, evaluation, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes automation trustworthy enough to leave running. I stream most of it, partly for accountability, mostly because it's more fun with people in chat.

Off-screen: chess, lifting, and an ongoing argument with my own note-taking system.
